TwoHa's Cafe

Type: 🧋 Coffee & Boba

Address: 123 Main St, Duluth, GA 30096

Hours: Mon-Sat 10am-8pm, Sun 11am-6pm

My Go-To Order

Sweet Corn Latte with oat milk — it's unique and delicious!

Menu Highlights

  • Specialty: Korean-inspired lattes
  • Popular Drinks: Sweet Corn Latte, Banana Cream Maple Latte, Brown Sugar Latte, Pistachio Latte
  • Milk Options: Dairy, Oat, Almond

Note: They also serve pastries, but I haven't tried them yet.
