Ding Tea

Type: 🧋 Boba / Tea

Address: 10900 Medlock Bridge Rd Ste 301, Johns Creek, GA 30097

Hours: 12:00 PM – 9:30 PM from Monday through Thursday. 11:30 AM - 10:00 PM on Friday and Saturday. 11:30 PM - 9:30 PM on Sunday.

My Go-To Order

My go-to order is the Oolong Milk Tea — smooth, roasted, tea flavor.

Another Oolong tea drink I know.

Menu Highlights

  • Specialty: Classic and creative bubble teas with a wide variety of options, including yakult and sparkling fruit juices. Their brown sugar series is a standout. They recently started offering coconut juice based drinks.
  • Popular Drinks (Top 5): Lychee Green Tea, Signature Milk Tea, Mango Slush, Signature Milk Tea Slush, and Guava Green Milk Tea.
  • Milk Options: Dairy, Oat, Almond, Soy.

📝 Note: They also serve Vietnamese sandwiches, Taiwanese chicken nuggets, and snacks like cheese coins and croffles.
