Glaze Tea

Type: 🧋 Boba

Address: 2205 Pleasant Hill Rd #125, Duluth, GA 30096

Hours: 12:00 PM–11:00 PM from Monday through Thursday, and Sunday. The hours on Friday and Saturday are 12:00 PM–12:00 AM.

My Go-To Order

My go-to order is the Milk Tea Hotpot — a shareable milk tea experience where you pick your base tea and up to six toppings. It's interactive, fun, and perfect for groups of 3–4 people.

You choose your tea base and sugar level, then add toppings like lychee jelly, boba, or pudding.

Mixing the tea and toppings in a cup was a unique experience.

Menu Highlights

  • Specialty: Glaze Tea's signature Milk Tea Hotpot turns milk tea into an interactive and flavorful dessert experience. You can customize it with your choice of tea base, sugar level, and up to six toppings. It's available in 400ml or 700ml sizes for $20–$23.
  • Popular Drinks (Top 5): Glaze 琉璃 (Camellia milk tea), Tropical, Blooming Jasmine, Purple Taro Taro, and Crème Brûlée.
  • Milk Options: Dairy, Oat, Almond, Soy.

📝 Note: They also serve snacks like salt and pepper chicken, but I haven't tried them yet.
