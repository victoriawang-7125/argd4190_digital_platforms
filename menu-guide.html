Drink Menu Guide

New to boba or coffee? Here's a quick overview. Check each spot's page to get a direct to their full menu.

🧋 Boba & Tea Basics

  • Tea Bases: Black, Green, Oolong, Fruit Tea
  • Popular Flavors: Taro, Mango, Matcha, Thai Tea, Brown Sugar
  • Toppings: Tapioca Pearls (chewy), Popping Boba (juice-filled), Jelly (soft), Pudding (smooth)
  • Customize: Sweetness (0-100%) and Ice (regular, less, none)

Tip: Sweetness is based on personal preference. Although some specialty drinks may work with a specific amount of sweetness.

☕ Coffee Basics

  • Espresso: Strong, concentrated shot
  • Latte: Espresso + steamed milk (creamy)
  • Americano: Espresso + hot water (lighter)
  • Cold Brew: Smooth, steeped in cold water
  • Iced Coffee: Brewed hot, then chilled
  • Flavor Add-ins: Vanilla, Caramel, Hazelnut, etc.
  • Milk Options: Dairy, Oat, Almond, Coconut

Tip: New to coffee? I recommend trying a latte for sweetness.

Boba vs Coffee: Quick Look

  • Boba: Tea-based, sweet, chewy toppings, afternoon treat
  • Coffee: Bean-based, bold/bitter, morning pick-me-up with caffeine
